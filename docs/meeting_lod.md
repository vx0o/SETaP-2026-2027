Main Purpose of the Meeting :
The purpose of the first meeting was to introduce the project, make sure all members understood the brief,
and begin organising how the team would work toghether, as well as preparing the project pitch.

What We Discussed : 
During the meeting we discussed the main idea behind the Group Work Contribution Manager.

We applied our discussion in to a Project Pitch to present in our practical session, and we assigned slides
so everyone could participate.
The system is intended to help members of a project team keep track of individual contributions. 
Team members will be able to add contributions they have made, and other members will be able to dispute contributions where necessary.
We also discussed the main requirements of the project and began considering what features the system may need.
The team also discussed how we would organise the project and communicate with each other.
